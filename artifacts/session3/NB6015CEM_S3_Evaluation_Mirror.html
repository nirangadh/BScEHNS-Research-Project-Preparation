Evaluation Design Mirror

[Illustrative example - do not cite] The worked design in this tool is Dilini's. Dilini is an invented student, and her project and its hospital are invented too.

One idea

An evaluation design needs a metric, a baseline and an environment, and all three should already be in your research question. This tool shows which of them it can find in your own two texts, and names one edit to try first.

Not a grader

This is a thinking mirror, not a grader. It reads your text for the surface signals of a testable design and reports what it can and cannot find. It cannot judge whether your metric is the right metric or whether your baseline is a fair comparison. A clean result here is not a mark and should not be over-trusted. Everything runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded, saved or sent anywhere.

Nothing leaves this page.

A worked evaluation design at the top band

Dilini, Suwa Sevana Teaching Hospital. [Illustrative example - do not cite]

Why this sits at the top band. It names a metric, a baseline and a controlled condition; it establishes ground truth deliberately rather than assuming it; it reports a secondary observation that would make a null result informative; and it states its own limits in terms of what claim the result will and will not support.
