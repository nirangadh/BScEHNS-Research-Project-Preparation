Responsible Practice Mirror

[Illustrative example - do not cite] The reference paragraph in this tool is written in the name of Tharindu. Tharindu, Chathura and Dilini are invented students, and their projects and organisations are invented too.

One idea

The law becomes specific when your method does. This tool compares two things that you write: what your method does, and what your legal, ethical and risk text says about it. The gap between them is the finding.

Not a grader

This is a thinking mirror, not a grader, and it is not legal advice. It cannot tell you whether your project is lawful or ethical, and it does not mark anything. The institutional ethics process is the authority for what is permitted. What it does is compare two things you write: what your method actually does, and what your responsible-practice text claims about it. The gap between them is the finding.

1. Your method, in your own words

What does the work touch, capture, run on, and produce? Three or four sentences is enough.

2. Your legal, ethical and risk writing

Paste the draft section as it currently stands, including your risk register if you have written one.
