Three Tells

[Illustrative example - do not cite] Chathura is an invented student, and Pahana Retail is an invented business. The baselines that his paragraphs name are real publications. What the paragraphs say he decided about them is invented.

One idea

A real justification shows three signs: alternatives are named, a test for choosing is stated, and the test comes from this project and not from the tool's reputation.

Not a grader

This tool does not read, judge or mark what you type. In the second part it only joins your words into two sentences and counts the parts you have filled. Whether the argument is sound is for you and your supervisor to decide.

Step 1. Choose a tell to look for

Step 2. Tap a part that shows it

Write your own, in the three-tell form

Take one tool or framework that you intend to use. Fill the four parts. The tool joins them with Chathura's own connecting words.

Name them, for example: "Tool X, Tool Y and Tool Z". The joined sentence continues "were considered."

Begin with "it", so that it follows the words "was selected because".

Write it so that it follows the words "which this project needs because".

Your justification

Read it aloud. If the last part would be equally true in any other project, it is a reputation and not a context. Go back to your project and look again.

What you type here stays on this device. It is never sent anywhere.
